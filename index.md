### How is climate change expected to impact traffic and transport in the Baltic Sea Region? 

Whether you operate at road, rail, or sea, this website provides information on changes in climatic conditions through variables that are the most relevant for your operations. Click a variable to open its page. Each variable is followed by icons of the affected transport mode and a short description.
